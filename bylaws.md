## How to format this file

- A line starting with `# ` is the page title.
- A line starting with `## ` starts a section and shows up in the section list at the top.
- A line starting with `### ` is a subsection.
- Lines starting with `- ` become bullet points, and lines starting with `1. ` become a numbered list.
- Put two stars on each side of words to make them bold.
- Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
