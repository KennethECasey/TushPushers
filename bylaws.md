# Tush Pushers Bylaws

Jan. 12, 2026

## League Setup

Classic 12 team Dynasty Superflex format.

- 22 man rosters (10 start 12 bench)
- Starters - 1qb, 2rb, 3wr, 1te, 2flex, 1superflex (Superflex may be QB,RB,WR,TE)
- 12 Bench spots
- 3 additional Injured Reserve Spots - (NFL IR and “out for season” designations only. No players who are out, doubtful, questionable, suspended, or otherwise not playing will be added to IR slots.)
- 3 additional taxi squad spots (essentially practice squad spots for rookies who are still developing. Players with more than 2 years of NFL experience are not eligible to stay on the practice squad. Once a player is removed from the taxi squad in season they may not return. Commissioners can return players from an active roster to taxi squad during season if asked to do so by team managers and so long as that player has not been active on the teams roster during an nfl game.)
- No D/ST or Kickers

## Points Scoring

- **Passing** - +0.04 per yard, +4 passing TD, +2 2pt conversions, -2 INT
- **Rushing** - +0.1 per yard, +6 rushing TD, +2 2pt conversions, -2 fumble lost
- **Receiving** - +1 PPR, +0.1 per yard, +6 receiving TD, +2 2pt conversions
- **Tie Breakers** - In the event of a tie the team with the highest scoring single bench player wins

## Startup Draft

The league startup draft will start at 12:30 PM CST on May 1st 2026, approximately a week after the end of the 2026 NFL rookie draft. (This date will be adjusted if sleeper has not yet added all rookies to the roster by May 1st). All active NFL players (rookies included) will be available to pick for the startup draft. It will be a snake draft format with a draft order to be determined at random at a later date. In order to accommodate every league member's schedule, the draft will be a 25 round slow draft with teams having up to 8 hours per pick. The draft clock will pause for 8 hours overnight between the hours of 12AM and 8AM CST. League members will not be able to select their draft picks during these hours. If a member runs out of time they will get a CPU autodraft for their pick. If any issues arise during the startup draft the commissioner will suspend the draft until the problem is resolved and the draft can resume.

## Rookie Drafts

Starting in 2027, drafts will be exclusively rookie picks. Rookie drafts will occur approximately one week after the NFL rookie draft for that class and last 4 rounds. These will be linear (not snake) drafts in order to help struggling teams. Rosters will be expanded to 26 men prior to the draft to accommodate rookies but must be cut back down to 22 a week before the start of the NFL season.

Draft order will be the inverse of the league standings from the prior season. (Based on playoff standings for top 6 teams and total wins and losses for bottom 6)

## Trades

### Draft Pick Trading

Startup draft picks are non transferable. Future draft picks (27,28, etc.) will not be available to trade until the end of the startup draft. Trading future draft picks up to three years out is allowed. However, dues must be paid in advance by both parties for any seasons affected by the trade. This is primarily to dissuade risky trades that may leave members wanting to drop out of the league in the future. There is a 14 day window to pay these dues during the off season. Dues for trades involving draft picks that happen during the season must be paid by both parties before the start of the next NFL game that is affected by that trade. (For example if a league member trades a 2028 first round pick for another player's receiver, both parties must pay the league dues for both 2027 and 2028 in order to keep the trade from being rescinded by the commissioner at the end of the deadline.) If only one party does not pay by the end of the deadline then the party who did not pay loses their third round rookie pick in the next year's draft to the party who did pay and the trade is rescinded. *(See Financials Section for more on league dues) (See League Misconduct section for more on enforcement and penalties)*

### Player Trades

Player trades will be enabled following the startup draft. There is a one day review period before trades are finalized. Any manager may choose to back out of a trade deal at any time before players have officially been added to new rosters and the deal is finalized. The commissioner has the ability to push trades through before this 24 hour period upon request. There is no trade veto but there are strict punishments for players who are found to be practicing collusion. *(See League Misconduct section for more on enforcement and penalties)*

### Trade Deadline

The trade deadline is week 12 of the NFL season. All trades must be accepted before the end of the final NFL game of week 12.

## Waivers

### FAAB System

This league will use a FAAB (Free Agent Acquisition Budget) bidding system for the waiver wire pickup system. Each member is given a budget of $100 (not real USD) in FAAB to bid on unclaimed players that are on waivers. Players will be claimed by the team with the highest FAAB bid. There is no minimum or maximum bid. FAAB budgets reset at the end of every season's fantasy playoffs as well as at the start of every NFL football season so there is no incentive to saving your FAAB for the next season. FAAB can be traded from one member to another. In season waivers clear every Wednesday at 2AM CST.

### Waiver Schedule

Waivers will clear daily during the off season with one exception. For three days after the end of the fantasy draft the commissioner will pause waivers temporarily in order to allow each member ample time to get familiar with the free agent market.

## Playoffs

Playoffs will be a 6 team 3 week format starting on week 15 and ending on week 17. Playoffs will be one week per round. The top two teams in the league standings (as well as the 7th and 8th ranked teams in the consolation bracket) will have a round 1 bye week. We are using default playoff seeding where teams stay on their initial side of the playoff bracket.

## Payouts and Awards

1st, 2nd, and 3rd place teams will all receive a payout following the end of the playoffs. The league champion will also receive some form of trophy (TBD) that will be passed along to the winners of the league season to season. No other players receive any monetary reward. The payout scale is as follows.

- **1st place** - 66.6% of the pot (8 payouts) $240
- **2nd place** - 25% of the pot (3 payouts) $90
- **3rd place** - 8.3% of the pot (1 payout)(recoups league dues) $30

**The Winner of the Consolation Bracket** - will receive an extra 2nd round draft pick at the end of the second round in the next season’s draft. This discourages tanking in the playoffs.

## Finances

### Buy-in

$30 for 2026 season. Each of the 12 members will pay their dues either in cash or via venmo/paypal. All league dues must be paid in full a week prior to the NFL Draft. This gives us two weeks to replace any member who chooses not to buy in. League member’s buy-in money will be placed in envelopes with their name on it and stored in a secure location throughout the duration of the season. Any buy-ins from future years that are results from draft pick trades will be placed into envelopes with the league members name on it as well as the season year that the buy-in is for. Projected league dues for future seasons will be based on current seasons buy in amount. *(See Trades Section)* League dues (including those for future seasons) are non-refundable and will not be returned if a player decides to drop out of the league at any time. Any funds remaining in a members account after they have left the league will be added to the prize pot as a bonus or used as an incentive to attract a replacement player for the following season who would then not have to pay that season's buy-in. Each year we will vote on if we should raise the buy in amount *(See League Democracy)*

### Stewardship of Funds

Being transparent about the league finances is of the utmost importance. As league commissioner I (Collin Gilreath) take sole responsibility for stewardship of league funds, collecting league dues, as well as paying out league prizes. While league dues are non-refundable, every member is entitled to proof that funds are being responsibly taken care of. If you have any concerns you may request an audit of the league bank in which I, at my earliest convenience, must produce proof (whether in person, over video, pictures, or otherwise) that all league funds are accounted for and organized properly. In the name of transparency I will also give an annual funds report shortly before our league draft to ensure that each member is aware of the financial state of our league and that there are no discrepancies. Should the league bank for any reason be compromised (Theft, Fire, etc.) I take sole responsibility and will pay out that year's league prizes from my personal funds. Should my handling of league funds come under scrutiny, league members may vote for a change of leadership. *(See League Democracy)*

## League Misconduct

### Collusion

Collusion is never acceptable in this league. Players may be accused of collusion if there is reason to believe that they have made trades or roster moves that are specifically designed to benefit one team at the expense of another. Instances of collusion will be put to an anonymous league wide vote where 100% of the players not accused in the collusion are required to vote against a member or members in order to unanimously find them guilty. Members choosing to abstain from voting in cases of collusion will be counted as guilty verdicts. Punishments for collusion are as follows.

- **1st offense** - Commissioner will rescind trade or undo action that is found to be an act of collusion and player(s) found guilty will lose their earliest third round pick for the next season.
- **2nd offense** - Commissioner will rescind trade or undo action that is found to be an act of collusion and player(s) found guilty will lose their earliest second round pick for the next season.
- **3rd offense** - Members league status will be put to vote. *(See League Democracy)* Should the member be allowed to stay in the league, the Commissioner will rescind trade or undo action that is found to be an act of collusion and player(s) found guilty will lose their earliest first round pick for the next season. Any offense after this is grounds for immediate termination from the league at the sole discretion of the commissioner.

There is no statute of limitations on collusion.

### Tanking

Tanking in this league is mostly legal. You are allowed to trade away any star players you may have for future picks or lower end pieces and try to lose every game to raise your draft stock. However, each member must make a “goodwill effort” to put out their best lineup every week. This does not mean that each member must always start any player who is projected to score more than any other player at that position. But a clear and significant downgrade at a single position is not acceptable. Say for instance you sit a star player (Josh Allen) to play a lower end player (Keon Coleman) at the superflex position in an obvious effort to tank. An act such as that would be grounds for a tanking vote. Members accused of tanking will be put on trial and a 73% vote (8 of 11 players not accused) must vote against a member for them to be found guilty of tanking illegally. Members abstaining from voting in cases of illegal tanking will be counted as guilty verdicts. The punishments for illegal tanking are as follows.

- **1st offense** - Loss of third round pick in next draft.
- **2nd offense** - Loss of second round pick in next draft.
- **3rd offense** - Loss of first round pick in next draft.

A single tanking offense resets at end of every season (if a player was found guilty twice in 2026 they will only carry one of those offenses over to the 2027 season)

### Financial Misconduct

If a member fails to pay the league dues before the deadline they are to be dropped from the league. They will have the opportunity to pay up until another individual takes their place in the league. In the event that the commissioner is accused of financial misconduct of any sort, there will be a vote of no confidence and a reelection procedure if he is impeached. *(See League Democracy)*

## League Democracy

### Amending the Bylaws

There are two main ways to amend the Bylaws: Adding and Changing.

**Adding** - The commissioner can add to and edit the wording (but not meaning) of the Bylaws only if he notices that something had not been considered or accounted for. He must notify the league of any changes or updates made to the bylaws within 24 hours. This can be done during the offseason at the discretion of the commissioner so long as he notifies the league. During the season any additions made by the commissioner must be run by the league with prior 24 hour notice giving members a chance to veto the addition unless urgent and vital to the integrity of the league. In order to veto a commissioner addition the league must have at least 3 people vote against the change… League members can also move that additions be made to the Bylaws during the off season. This movement must be seconded by another member at which time the addition will be put to a vote. In order to be ratified, the addition must pass the vote with an 83%(10 of 12) approval rating or better.

**Changing** - Changing of any league Bylaws can ONLY be done during the off season Prior to the league’s season draft. During this time members can move to change standing league Bylaws and policies. These movements need to be seconded by at least one other member to be put to a vote. All changes to standing Bylaws and policies can only be ratified by a unanimous vote in favor of changing.

### Expelling Members

Members can be nominated by other members for expulsion due to repeated acts of misconduct and are automatically nominated if they are found guilty of collusion three times. The League Commissioner may also nominate any player for expulsion ONLY if they feel as though a member is seriously threatening the integrity of the league. All league members not implicated in the scandal must vote for the expulsion of a member in order to effectively expel a member from the league. If expelled from the league an individual is not eligible to rejoin for two full seasons even if a spot becomes available. Members expelled from the league are not eligible to receive any prizes or awards as a result of their teams playoff performances.

### Orphan Teams

When a member is expelled, drops out, or is otherwise unable to participate in the league, their team becomes an “orphan team”. If a team is orphaned during the season the league commissioner will make lineup decisions for that team for the remainder of the season. In the event that an Orphan team wins a monetary prize, the prize is given to the next best performing team and subsequent prizes also get awarded further down the rankings. After the season is finished, a new member will be assigned to the orphan team. If that team has a win percentage of less than 40%, the new member will be granted an extra draft pick at the end of the first round as an incentive to join the league and as a bonus for taking on a struggling team.

### Impeachment of League Commissioner / Vote of No Confidence

Should the league commissioner break the Bylaws, take advantage of his position, or otherwise engage in misconduct detrimental to the integrity of the league, members have the right to move to impeach the commissioner in a vote of no confidence. This motion must be seconded by another member to be put to vote. 8 of 11 of the remaining members of the league must vote no confidence in order to impeach a league commissioner. Impeaching a commissioner does not remove them from the league but, should a commissioner be impeached, they will automatically be nominated for expulsion from the league. If not expelled, the disgraced commissioner will not get a vote in league votes for an entire year to the date that he was impeached. If the majority of league members deem it necessary they can also vote for an additional punishment of up to but not exceeding the loss of a single second round draft pick.

### Election of a New Commissioner

Should there be a vacancy at commissioner, whether due to impeachment, retirement, death, or otherwise; the league will hold an election for the new commissioner. Up to four members may run for election and each must have at least one other member officially and publicly supporting their candidacy. Once candidates have been established, all league members (candidates included) will vote for the new commissioner. Whoever has the popular vote wins. If there is a tie, keep voting until someone comes out on top.

### Annual Buy-in Adjustment

At the end of every season there will be a vote on whether or not to raise the buy-in for the next season. In order to raise league buy-in amounts, members must vote at least 75% (9 of 12) in favor of raising the amount. The commissioner will then conduct a survey to see what average price the members would like to adjust the buy-in to. The amount may not raise more than $20 season to season. The amount may not fall season to season unless amended during the off season. Should the price be raised during the off season, members who have paid future dues in advance as a result of draft trading will only be responsible for paying the amount that will bring their accounts for that season equal to the league's required buy-in. They will have until the buy-in deadline to bring their account to the proper balance.

### Voting

All voting will be done anonymously either through a google form or using the sleeper app poll function. Results of votes will be shared by the commissioner at the end of the voting period. Members are not required to vote on every subject. Voting results (aside from commissioner elections or otherwise specified) are based on the number of votes and not percentages of collected votes. If every vote is for something but there are insufficient votes, that vote is unsuccessful. All voting periods are to be at least 24 hours long in order allow all members time to consider their vote and cast it. In cases where there are time constraints due to extenuating circumstances, the commissioner will allow for as much time as practicality possible for members to vote on issues.

### Emergency Measures

The commissioner of the league is tasked with the responsibility of maintaining a smoothly functioning league and upholding its rules and values. There may come a time when in order to meet this obligation, and in order to uphold the best interests of the league, a commissioner may need to take action outside of his powers explicitly stated within these Bylaws. Should these circumstances arise the commissioner must immediately notify the league by declaring an official state of emergency. The commissioner must explain to league members what the issue is and what steps they are taking or have taken to solve it as well as explain how these steps are outside of their explicitly stated power as league commissioner. Emergency actions are more difficult for league members to veto than regular commissioner additions and require a majority vote (6 of 11) against the action in order to veto it. Should any members suspect that the commissioner is taking advantage of their power to enact a state of emergency in order to benefit themselves or in any other way take advantage of the system it is those members responsibility to call for a vote of no confidence.
